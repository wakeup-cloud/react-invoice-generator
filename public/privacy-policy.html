At Make Invoice Online (accessible from https://makeinvoiceonline.com), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Make Invoice Online and how we use it.

1. Client-Side Data Processing (Privacy First)

Make Invoice Online is designed as a client-side web application. All data you enter into the invoice form—including company names, address details, client info, itemized charges, and uploaded logos—is processed locally within your web browser. We do not transmit, collect, or store your sensitive invoice details on our servers.

2. Information We Collect

Because all processing happens locally, we do not require account registration, passwords, or personal email details to use our core invoice generation service.

Like many other web applications, standard, non-personally identifiable browser data may be logged by web hosting environments (such as Cloudflare Pages or web servers) for operational security, diagnostics, and performance analysis. This may include:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses
  • Browser type and version
  • Referring / exit pages
  • Date and time stamps

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We do not set tracking cookies to build personal user profiles. We may utilize browser local storage (HTML5 LocalStorage) purely to save your invoice preferences or session state locally on your device so you do not lose your work if you refresh the page.

4. Third-Party Web Analytics and Services

We may use privacy-compliant third-party web analytics tools (such as Google Analytics or Cloudflare Web Analytics) to analyze high-level traffic patterns and improve user experience. These tools collect aggregate statistics (such as total page visits and general country demographics) without identifying individual users.

5. Security of Your Information

Because your sensitive financial data remains inside your browser and is never uploaded to an external database, the risk of server-side data breaches is eliminated. We employ standard HTTPS encryption across our website to ensure all connection channels remain secure.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@makeinvoiceonline.com.
