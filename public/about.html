About Make Invoice Online

Welcome to Make Invoice Online, your trusted free web utility for generating clean, professional, and customizable PDF invoices in seconds.

Our Mission

Managing business finances shouldn't be complicated or expensive. We created Make Invoice Online to empower freelancers, contractors, consultants, and small business owners with a hassle-free tool to handle billing efficiently. Our goal is to eliminate monthly software subscriptions and lengthy sign-up procedures for basic invoicing needs.

Why Privacy Matters to Us

Unlike traditional online billing platforms, Make Invoice Online operates with a privacy-first approach. All invoice computations, PDF rendering, and client details are processed entirely in your local web browser. Your private billing records and financial figures are never saved on or transmitted to external servers.

Free Forever

We are committed to keeping our core PDF invoice generator 100% free for individual and commercial use worldwide, supported by subtle display advertising.
